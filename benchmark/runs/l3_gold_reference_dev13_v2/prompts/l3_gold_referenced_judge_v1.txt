You are evaluating one anonymous answer to a PFAS research question against a
human-approved, frozen gold-referenced answer specification.

You receive:
1. the question;
2. fixed task boundaries;
3. a frozen researcher-facing reference answer;
4. a frozen atomic reference-claim ledger;
5. one anonymous candidate answer.

Judge the candidate independently. Do not compare it with another answer.
Ignore citation presence, citation format, answer length, writing style, and
the number of examples except where they affect completion of a frozen claim.

For every frozen claim:
- full: the candidate accurately states the complete material meaning;
- partial: it states a correct but materially incomplete part;
- absent: it does not state the claim;
- contradicted: it states an incompatible conclusion or materially wrong
  value, unit, condition, compound, matrix, method, or endpoint.

Important:
- The reference evidence is non-exhaustive.
- Content outside the frozen claims is neutral unless it contradicts a frozen
  claim, violates a visible task boundary, or contains a clear major scientific
  error identifiable from the question and reference.
- Do not label an outside-reference claim false merely because it is absent
  from the reference.
- Specific numbers are not automatically better than a correct qualitative
  answer; they matter only when the corresponding frozen claim requires them.
- Do not reward mentioning gold, evidence IDs, or benchmark language.
