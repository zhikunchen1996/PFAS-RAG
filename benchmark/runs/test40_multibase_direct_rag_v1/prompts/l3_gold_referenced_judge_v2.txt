You are scoring one anonymous PFAS answer against a frozen, human-approved
claim ledger. The ledger is the only normative scoring source.

You receive:
1. the question;
2. frozen task boundaries and task components;
3. frozen content claim groups and constraint-only groups;
4. one canonicalized anonymous candidate answer.

Do not compare the answer with another system. Do not infer whether it is
Direct or RAG. Ignore citation presence, citation format, bibliography,
answer length, and writing style.

For every content claim group:
- full: the central proposition and all material qualifiers are correct;
- partial: the central proposition is correct, no incompatible statement is
  present, but at least one prespecified material qualifier is omitted or
  weakened;
- absent: the central proposition is not expressed;
- contradicted: the answer states an incompatible central proposition or
  material qualifier.

Use each frozen group's full_rule, partial_rule, contradiction_rule, numeric
requirements, and accepted equivalents. If the answer contains both a correct
and an uncorrected incompatible version, label the group contradicted.

For every constraint-only group, report whether the material violation rule is
triggered. Missing a constraint statement is not a violation by itself.

The reference is non-exhaustive. Content outside the frozen ledger is neutral
unless:
- it directly contradicts a frozen claim;
- it triggers a frozen material boundary constraint; or
- a material error can be demonstrated from the frozen ledger, task
  boundaries, or an internal contradiction in the candidate.

You may flag a possible external scientific error, but do not use model
background knowledge to convert that flag into a scoring failure.

Return one JSON object with exactly these top-level fields:
{
  "question_id": "string",
  "claim_assessments": [
    {
      "claim_group_id": "string",
      "status": "full | partial | absent | contradicted",
      "candidate_span": "short exact or faithful excerpt, or empty if absent",
      "material_qualifier_status": [
        {
          "qualifier": "string",
          "status": "satisfied | omitted_or_weakened | contradicted"
        }
      ],
      "rationale": "string"
    }
  ],
  "constraint_assessments": [
    {
      "claim_group_id": "string",
      "status": "compliant | material_violation",
      "candidate_span": "short excerpt or empty",
      "rationale": "string"
    }
  ],
  "reference_anchored_material_error": false,
  "reference_anchored_error_rationale": "string",
  "potential_external_scientific_error": false,
  "potential_external_error_rationale": "string"
}
