You are scoring one anonymous PFAS professional review against a frozen,
candidate-blind task ledger. Evaluate the scientific usefulness of the review
text, including how evidence is cited and integrated, not its retrieval-system
label or prose polish.

You receive:
1. the visible review brief and its three required components;
2. frozen task boundaries and material-violation rules; and
3. one canonicalized anonymous candidate review.

Citation identifiers have been deterministically anonymized to neutral
`[R##]` markers before this request. Preserve and evaluate citation use, but do
not infer the producing system. Do not award citations merely for being
numerous, and do not reward answer length, headings, or writing style.
Evidence discovery, field verification, metadata-based reference quality,
source-breadth counts, and citation entailment are verified in Layer A.
Layer B must assess whether major source-dependent claims are visibly cited,
whether citation placement makes the supported claim identifiable, and
whether multiple nonredundant sources support distinct parts of the reasoning.
Meaningful evidence breadth may earn credit; raw marker count alone may not.
Do not reward isolated numbers, decorative citations, or repeated citation of
one source.

This judgment has two levels.

LEVEL 1: REQUIRED-COMPONENT FULFILLMENT

For each frozen component, assign one status:

- full: directly answers the complete requirement and reaches a usable,
  appropriately bounded conclusion;
- partial: addresses the requirement meaningfully but omits one material part,
  condition, comparison, or conclusion;
- absent: provides only a passing mention, generic background, or no relevant
  answer;
- contradicted: materially reverses the component or gives an internally
  incompatible answer.

Judge fulfillment only. Do not require every component to contain its own
future-research discussion; outlook is assessed globally below.

LEVEL 2: GLOBAL REVIEW OPERATIONS

Assess each atomic operation across the complete review. Use the same scale:

- full: the operation is clearly and materially performed;
- partial: it is present but incomplete, weakly connected, or limited to a
  small part of the task;
- absent: it is not materially performed;
- contradicted: the review performs the operation in a way that materially
  conflicts with its own account or the frozen task boundaries.

Critical synthesis operations

1. comparative_integration
   Integrates findings across alternatives, studies, conditions, or evidence
   types. A catalog or study-by-study list is not full.
2. conditional_explanation
   Explains why outcomes differ using relevant mechanisms, matrices,
   compounds, operating conditions, scale, or analytical scope.
3. tension_limitation_resolution
   Identifies and resolves or appropriately qualifies material tensions,
   trade-offs, comparability limits, or failure modes.
4. decision_conclusion
   Derives a conditional conclusion that follows from the synthesis and is
   useful for the decision posed by the task.

Professional-depth operations

1. mechanism_specificity
   Uses technically specific mechanisms rather than labels alone.
2. context_boundary_specificity
   Preserves material PFAS, matrix, concentration, operating-condition, scale,
   and analytical-scope boundaries where relevant.
3. empirical_data_use
   Uses condition-bound empirical observations, quantitative results, ranges,
   or study-level contrasts to support the review's reasoning. Full requires
   empirical information to do analytical work, not merely appear as isolated
   numbers. Do not verify citations or require a fixed number of values.
4. endpoint_comparability_discipline
   Keeps removal, separation, adsorption, transformation, degradation,
   defluorination, mineralization, and destruction distinct and avoids direct
   comparison of materially incompatible endpoints or conditions.
5. practitioner_decision_support
   Converts the analysis into an actionable framework, conditional rule,
   prioritized measurement, or operational implication.
6. citation_evidence_integration
   Connects source-dependent empirical, quantitative, comparative, and
   mechanistic claims to appropriately placed citations and integrates
   nonredundant sources into the reasoning. Full requires that major
   source-dependent claims are generally cited, citations are placed so their
   associated claims are identifiable, and multiple sources materially support
   different findings, comparisons, or limitations in the analytical
   structure. Penalize substantial uncited source-dependent claims, citation
   dumping, decorative citation, or reliance on one citation for materially
   different claims. Do not infer entailment, source authenticity, or truth
   from a marker alone; Layer A performs that verification.

Current-research and outlook operations

1. current_development_specificity
   Identifies concrete emerging methods, analytical developments, deployment
   evidence, or changes in the state of research rather than saying only that
   the field is evolving.
2. specific_gap
   States a precise unresolved scientific or implementation gap.
3. consequence_of_gap
   Explains which interpretation, design choice, or deployment decision the
   gap prevents or weakens.
4. testable_priority
   Proposes a testable, measurable, or operationally specific next step and
   gives a defensible priority rationale.

CLAIM CALIBRATION

Assess the complete review:

- consistent: certainty, scope, and endpoint language remain internally
  consistent;
- minor_inconsistency: a localized mismatch does not materially change a
  professional conclusion;
- material_inconsistency: an absolute or unqualified claim conflicts with the
  review's own qualifications, evidence hierarchy, endpoint distinctions, or
  stated limitations and could change a professional conclusion;
- not_assessable: the answer contains too little substantive content.

Quote the shortest decisive span for an inconsistency. Do not use outside
knowledge to invent an error.

BOUNDARY CONSTRAINTS

For each frozen boundary, report whether its material-violation rule is
triggered. Missing a preferred statement is not a violation. The local scorer
applies deterministic caps for material inconsistency and boundary violations.

The ledger is non-exhaustive. Content outside it is neutral unless it
materially undermines a required component, triggers a frozen boundary, or is
demonstrably inconsistent with the visible task, ledger, or review itself.

Return one JSON object following the supplied schema exactly. Do not return an
overall score, dimension score, or system preference; all numeric aggregation
is performed locally.
