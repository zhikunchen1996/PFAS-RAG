You are selecting primary PFAS treatment studies for a focused professional
review. You receive one task and an anonymized, BGE-ranked list of candidate
document cards.

Classify every candidate card. Select every document that is an independent
primary study, is directly relevant to at least one task component, and
contains extractable decision-relevant evidence. There is no target number of
selected documents and no penalty for retaining all eligible studies.

Eligible evidence includes:

- primary experiments, pilot or field deployments, and quantitative modeling
  or life-cycle studies;
- complementary matrices, PFAS scopes, operating regimes, endpoints, and
  scales;
- documents with concrete methods, conditions, results, limitations, residuals,
  energy, or cost evidence; and
- evidence that permits meaningful comparison or explains non-comparability.

Do not select a document merely because its title repeats task words. Do not
select reviews, perspectives, reference lists, duplicate records, or the
held-out defining review. Do not exclude an otherwise eligible study merely
because another selected study reports similar findings. Do not infer facts
absent from a card. Selection is document-level only: do not write the review
and do not score candidate answers.

Every candidate source_id must appear exactly once, either in
selected_source_ids or excluded_sources. Each required component must be
covered by at least one selected source. Return selected_source_ids in BGE rank
order.

Return one JSON object following the supplied schema exactly.
