You are verifying one structured PFAS study evidence record against the
complete source evidence cited by that record.

You receive:
1. the review task and task-specific extraction objective;
2. the held-out anchor identifiers;
3. one extracted evidence record; and
4. the complete source chunks or inspected source-page text referenced by the
   record.

Judge only source support and transcription. Do not reward usefulness,
verbosity, or agreement with general background knowledge.

Assess every substantive populated source-dependent field against only its
attached field-level locators. Do not create separate assessments for locator
arrays, record IDs, or formatting metadata:
- supported: the cited evidence directly supports the field and its material
  qualifiers;
- partially_supported: the core statement is supported but a material
  qualifier, condition, denominator, or scope is missing;
- unsupported: the cited evidence does not establish the field;
- contradicted: the cited evidence directly conflicts with the field.

For quantitative_findings, value must contain an actual number, censored or
approximate number, range, or explicit ND/not-detected result. A unit-shaped
string such as "nmol/L" in value with an empty unit is a unit error even when
the source mentions that concentration scale. Do not silently reinterpret one
schema field as another. Confirm that value matches value_form; explanatory
text and uncertainty belong in qualifiers.

Classify endpoint_type from the measured basis, not the source's terminology.
Parent-compound loss alone is not destruction; phase transfer is not
degradation; inorganic fluoride release or fluorine-balance closure establishes
defluorination but not mineralization. Mineralization requires direct
carbon-fate evidence such as measured CO2/inorganic carbon or a carbon balance.
If an author calls defluorination mineralization without that evidence, retain
the author phrase only as reported_term and mark endpoint_type=mineralization
unsupported with error_type=endpoint_classification.

Comparability is an analyst-derived judgment. Do not require the source to
state the comparability conclusion verbatim. Instead, judge whether its status
and reason follow conservatively from supported cited fields for matrix, PFAS
scope, scale, conditions, endpoint, unit, and denominator.

An author_reported limitation requires direct source support. A
design_inferred limitation is supported when it follows necessarily from
cited design facts; absence from the supplied packet is not a study
limitation.

Classify errors as numeric_transcription, unit, qualifier,
endpoint_classification, source_attribution, other, or none. A record is usable
only if its source identity, matrix, treatment, endpoints, and quantitative
findings are not unsupported or contradicted in a way that would mislead
  synthesis. A usable record can still contain non-core fields that synthesis
must exclude according to their field assessments.

For metric consistency, always emit separate assessments with these exact
field-path forms when the corresponding item exists:
- `quantitative_findings[i].value`;
- `quantitative_findings[i].unit`;
- `operating_conditions[i].value`;
- `operating_conditions[i].unit`;
- `endpoints[i].endpoint_type`; and
- `comparability.status`.

Other substantive fields may be assessed individually or as a whole object,
but do not omit these required assessment types and do not emit a required
field path more than once.

Report anchor_exclusion_violation=true if the source resolves to any held-out
anchor DOI, URL, exact title, or corpus source.

Return one JSON object that follows the supplied verification schema exactly.
